About Me

My name is Nikki McNeill, and I am an educator with a passion for helping students grow as readers, writers, thinkers, and people. My work in education has allowed me to support students, collaborate with teachers, lead school initiatives, and continue growing as a professional.

I am especially interested in curriculum design, instructional coaching, library and information science, educational technology, and creating school communities where students feel connected and supported.
