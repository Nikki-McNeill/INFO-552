Language Support

Resources and tips for families needing language support at school.

Translation Services

Families can request all translated documents from the main office in a language you understand.

  • Ask the main office how to request translated documents.
  • Keep a list of school apps and websites that offer language settings.
  • Use trusted translation tools for quick understanding, but ask the school for official help when needed.

Interpreter Information

It is your right to have an interpreter available during meetings, phone calls, or conferences.

  • Ask for an interpreter before a meeting.
  • Share the language or dialect needed.
  • Ask questions until the information is clear.

Phone Interpreters

Schools can use phone interpreters to help families communicate in their preferred language. Families can ask for an interpreter when calling or visiting the school.

Multilingual Resources

The Delaware Department of Education requires that the state adopted Home Language Survey is included in all enrollment packets. The Home Language Survey (HLS) is available in multiple languages.

Free Apps

Free translation apps can help with quick communication. Families should still request official support for important school decisions.

  • Google Translate
  • DeepL
  • Reverso Context

Helpful phrase

“I need an interpreter, please.”

Families can show this sentence to school staff when they need spoken language help.
