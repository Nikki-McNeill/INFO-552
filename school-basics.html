School Basics

A simple guide to attendance, grades, communication, and everyday middle school routines.

Attendance

Students are expected to attend school every day unless they are sick or have an approved reason. Families should report absences as soon as possible.

  • Call the attendance office or email the school attendance office.
  • Send a note when the student returns.
  • Ask your child's teacher for missed work if the student is absent for more than one day.

Families can use the Appoquinimink Attendance Excuse Form

Long Term Leave

For students who relocate out of state for any time longer than a one week vacation, the parent/guardian must provide documentation that the student has relocated out of state and the student must be withdrawn. The parent/guardian must re-enroll the student in the District once the family returns to the area. The school cannot ensure that the student will be able to be placed back in the original classroom.

Grading System

Grading criteria include oral and written assessments including tests, projects, reports, presentations, portfolios and homework. Parents should direct questions concerning grading to the teacher.

The scale is:

A+ = 97-100 A = 93-96 A- = 90-92

B+ = 87-89 B = 83-86 B- = 80-82

C+ = 77-79 C = 73-76 C- = 70-72

D+ = 67-69 D = 63-66 D- = 60-62

F = 59 and below

To pass the school year, students must reach a minimum of 240 points adding all marking period grades together.

Be Sure to:

  • Check grades weekly on Infinite Campus.
  • Look for missing assignments.
  • Email the teacher when you have a question.

Parent Communication

Teachers will primarily communicate through email and phone calls. Please be sure to place any addresses relating to @appo.k12.de.us on the safe senders' list to ensure you receive all communications from the school. Sometimes, the school and district will utilize text messages, paper letters, and their school and district websites or social media pages to communicate. Be sure to keep your email, address, and phone number up to date with the school by contacting the front office.

Ask the school which tools are most important for your student.
