Activities & Clubs

Information about school activities, sports, music, and clubs for families.

Why activities matter

Clubs, sports, music, and after-school programs help students build friendships and feel more connected to school.

Questions to ask

  • What clubs are available?
  • Is there a fee?
  • Does my student need a physical for sports?
  • How will my student get home after the activity?

Sports

Our district offers a wide variety of sports throughout the school year!

FALL: Girls Volleyball, Cross Country, Field Hockey

WINTER: Basketball

SPRING: Track & Field, Boys Volleyball

Students interested in trying out for any sport should visit Eagles Sports Page for details, including the DIAA Physical Form, which is required for all athletes.

Music and Arts

Drum Line Club

Our Drum Line Club is an exciting part of the LLRMS music program, bringing energy, rhythm, and school spirit to life! Members develop their percussion skills while performing at sporting events, pep rallies, and even in local parades throughout the year.

Drum Line is a great way to build musical talent, teamwork, and school pride!

Club Advisor:Jeffrey Leager

Art Club:Our Art Club is a creative haven for students who love to express themselves through various art forms. From painting and drawing to sculpture and mixed media, our club offers a supportive environment for budding artists to explore their talents and share their work with the school community.

Interested in joining Orchestra, Band, or Choir? Reach out to the school counselor to ensure your child has the classes they need for related arts.
