Transportation

Information about school transportation options and tips for families.

Bus Information

Families will receive an email notification about their student's bus route in late August. Your child's school can, upon request, provide a hard copy of the information. If you have a childcare transportation request, please contact the office staff at your student's school. *If your email address has changed recently, please contact the front office at your student's school.

  • Confirm the bus stop before the first day.
  • Arrive early at the stop.
  • Contact transportation if the bus is very late.
  • Complete the Bus Transportation Request Form if your family needs to request transportation support.
  • Complete the Transportation Request Form in Spanish if your family prefers the Spanish version.

Closing and Dismissals

During inclement weather or emergency situations, early dismissal/school closing information for the Appoquinimink School District will be available from the following:

Automated Phone Messages

The district employs an automated phone messaging system to help alert parents. To ensure its effectiveness, it is crucial that we have the correct phone numbers for your family. Please be sure to check the information currently on file, and supply updates to the office at your home school whenever changes occur.

Text Messages

If your family has a smart phone whose number on file with the school district, you will receive an automatic email announcement.

State School Closing Website and Email Notification

Parents can register for email notification and/or view the status of school closings across the state. Go to: http://schoolclosings.delaware.gov
