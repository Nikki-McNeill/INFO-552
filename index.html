Family Compass

A resource hub for multilingual families navigating middle school in the United States.

Welcome

Middle school can feel confusing when the system is new, the language is new, and the information is spread across too many places. Family Compass brings key school and community information together in one simple place.

This site is designed for families with students in grades 6-8 who need clear, friendly guidance about school expectations, transportation, technology, language support, activities, and community resources.

Quick-start checklist

  • Learn how attendance and grading work.
  • Find transportation and arrival information.
  • Set up parent portal and school communication apps.
  • Ask about translation, interpretation, and language support.
  • Explore clubs, sports, tutoring, and community help.

Start here

If your family is new to middle school in the United States, begin with the basics. Learn how attendance, grades, school communication, transportation, and support services usually work.

You do not need to understand everything at once. Start with one topic, then come back when you need more help.
