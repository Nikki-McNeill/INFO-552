Food Assistance

Free Summer Meals are back​!

​​​​

Thanks to support from the USDA and local agent the State of Delaware, the Appoquinimink School District is pleased to once again provide a Free Summer Meals Program at Louis L. Redding Middle School, 201 New Street, Middletown, DE.

Tasty, nutritious lunches will be served at no cost to any young person between the ages of 1-18.

The program will operate Monday–Thursday, from 11:30 a.m. until 12:30 p.m.

No registration, or student identification is needed. Participants are asked to consume the meal in the cafeteria.

The dates for the Free Summer Meals program are June 15, 2026 – August 13, 2026.

What: Free Summer Meals for young people between the ages of 1-18

When: Monday, June 15, 2026 - Thursday, August 13, 2026

Where: Louis L. Redding Middle School, 201 New Street, Middletown, DE 19709

Questions: Appoquinimink School Nutrition Office, 302-376-4280

For information about other sites: Call 1-866-3-HUNGRY or go to https://www.fns.usda.gov/meals4kids

Healthcare

Redding Middle School is the only middle school in the district with a school-based dental clinic.

Sign up for dental appointments at the clinic by a Delaware licensed dentist.

Spanish Redding Middle School Dental Clinic Sign Up Form

Can my child receive dental care?

Child must be a student at Louis L. Redding Middle School

Child must be insured by Delaware Medicaid, CHIP or have no dental insurance

Have not had a dental cleaning in the past 7 months

Please note: you will not receive a bill for any dental service or oral health product

Housing Support

Kinship Care Program

The Kinship Care Program provides financial assistance for relative caregivers during the 180-day transition period when a child first moves into the non-parent caregiver's home.

Call 1-866-843-7212 for more information.

Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF)

The Needy Family Fund helps Delaware's children and families in desperate need stave off hunger and homelessness.

Call 1-302-696-3120 or email ldisantis@delawareonline.com to get more info

Family Services Program

Family Services assists those in crisis, helping them meet their immediate, basic needs for food, clothing, heat, electricity, diapers, school supplies, and co-pays.

Call 1-302-378-7217

Mentoring Services

Students With Mentors:

Stay in school and aspire to, or achieve better grades (76%)

Are less likely than their peers to skip a day of school and less likely to skip a class

Develop more positive attitudes toward school (85%)

Feel better about themselves and have more self confidence (87%)

Improve their relationships with adults (65%)

Are better able to express their feelings (76%)

AND

The vast majority of our mentors (97%) report that they enjoyed the experience and found it very rewarding.
