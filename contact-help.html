Contact & Help

Information about who to contact for help and how to submit a help request.

Who can help?

  • Main office: General questions, attendance, records, and school procedures.
  • Counselor: Student schedules, emotional support, and family concerns.
  • Teacher: Grades, assignments, and classroom questions.
  • District MLL Student Support Services: Translation, interpretation, testing, and support.
