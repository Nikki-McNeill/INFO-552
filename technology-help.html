Technology Help

Resources and tips for families needing technology support at school.

Device Support

Students will receive a school-issued iPad for classwork, homework, and communication. If the device is broken, lost, or not working, please have your student complete an Eagle Tech Ticket for support. Submit a Tech Ticket

Parent Portals

Parents and guardians can use the Infinite Campus parent portals to check grades, attendance, schedules, and messages. Save your username and password in a safe place. Click on the link below to set up and access the portal. Access Parent Portal
